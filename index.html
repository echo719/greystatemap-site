What the game is

Rules of Engagement: The Grey State is a free-to-play PvPvE extraction shooter for PC from Grey State Studio, an internal Tencent Games studio. Formerly Aurora Studio, the team behind Ring of Elysium. It was announced on 15 October 2025.

You play a Strider, an operative hired to enter the Grey State — an unstable parallel dimension the developers call a “Horrorverse” — in squads of 3. You scavenge gear, fight monsters, and decide whether to take a safe low-value extraction or push for The Vertex and fight rival squads at the final exfil.
