Playtest access

There is no open playtest right now. The studio has said sign-up information goes out through its Discord — that is the only confirmed route, and it is free.

Be careful. Pre-release extraction shooters attract key-selling scams. There is no paid access to this game — it is free to play. Anyone charging for a key or an “alpha slot” is not selling you anything real.

The two confirmed steps

  1. Wishlist on Steam. The studio's own Steam announcement asks players to wishlist and join the Discord for playtest information. Wishlisting also means Steam notifies you the moment a playtest build or release date goes live.
  2. Join the official Discord. This is where the studio said sign-up details will be posted. Link is in the sources below — we link to the official invite only.

What is not confirmed

  • No playtest date has been given.
  • No region restrictions have been published.
  • No NDA terms have been published.
  • No information on whether playtest progress carries over to launch.

We will fill these in as they are announced rather than guessing now.

What we will do the day a playtest opens

This site's interactive map and class pages are deliberately shipped as empty shells. The moment a build is playable we start marking extraction points, loot spawns and monster locations on Boreas Isle, and recording the actual talent trees. If you want to help mark up the map, the contact address is on the about page.
