Release date: what is confirmed and what is not

Short answer: there is no release date. The Steam store still reads “To be announced”. Anyone giving you a specific day is guessing.

Why the 2026 figure keeps appearing

Grey State Studio said “2026” in its announcement materials, and press coverage has repeated that ever since. A year is not a date. Until the Steam store field changes from “To be announced” to a real day, the honest answer is that nobody outside the studio knows.

What would actually signal a date

  1. The Steam store release-date field changing from “To be announced”.
  2. An alpha or beta playtest opening — extraction shooters have almost always run a public test before launch, and the studio has already said playtest information will go out through Discord.
  3. A dated trailer. The most recent gameplay trailer landed on 30 July 2026 and carried no date.

We check all three and update the status board when any of them moves.
