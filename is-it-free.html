Is it free to play?

Yes. It is listed as free to play on Steam, on PC only, at launch.

The confirmed position

PriceFree to play
Steam “is free” flagYes
StorefrontsPC — Steam and Epic Games Store
Console versionNone announced

What the studio has said about monetisation

Press coverage from the announcement describes a “fair-to-play” model focused on cosmetic items. That phrasing comes from the studio's own announcement materials, and we are quoting it rather than endorsing it — no store, no battle pass and no price list has been shown yet.

What has not been confirmed

  • Whether there is a battle pass, and whether it is seasonal.
  • Whether stash space, loadout slots or character slots are purchasable.
  • Whether any progression can be bought.
  • Regional pricing for cosmetics.

“Cosmetics only” is a claim a lot of extraction shooters have made and then adjusted. We will record what actually ships.

Age rating and content

Steam lists the content descriptors as: Frequent Violence or Gore; General Mature Content. The game supports English, German, Russian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish, with full audio in English and Simplified Chinese.
